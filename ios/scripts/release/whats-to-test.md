Welcome to the first Octo beta for iPhone.

Octo plays your own music: your server (Navidrome, any Subsonic-compatible server, or Octo) and the songs already on your iPhone.

Please try:

- Sign in to your server (Library, then Sign in to a server), and play an album, a playlist and a station.
- Add the Home Screen widgets, and find Octo in the Shortcuts app and in Siri.
- Download a playlist and play it with no connection.
- Open the lyrics in the player.
- Try Automix (Settings, Playback, Between songs) and a theme (Settings, Appearance).

Something not right? Take a screenshot in Octo to send it as TestFlight feedback, or open an issue at github.com/winters27/octo.
